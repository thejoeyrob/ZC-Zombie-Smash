Zombie Smash – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Summary

Zombie Smash has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. The only information that leaves your device is what the online leaderboard needs.

What is sent to our server

  • Player name – the name you type when you start a run. It is shown on the public leaderboard, so do not use your real name or personal details.
  • Score and run details – your score, wave reached and related game results.
  • Random device identifier – a random code the game generates on your device the first time it runs. It is not your advertising ID or any hardware ID. It is used to stop duplicate and cheated scores.

This data is stored with our leaderboard provider, Supabase, and is used only to run the leaderboard and keep it fair.

What stays on your device

Settings, your best scores and the cached leaderboard are saved in the app's local storage. Deleting the app removes them.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing your data with third parties for marketing.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

The game does not ask for personal information. Leaderboard names are limited to letters, numbers and simple punctuation.

Removing your data

To have your leaderboard name and scores removed, open an issue at github.com/thejoeyrob/ZC-Zombie-Smash/issues with the player name you used. Do not post anything private in the issue.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
