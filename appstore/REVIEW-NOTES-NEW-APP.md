Zombie Smash, version 1.0

Background: this app record replaces an earlier one for the same bundle ID (com.thejoeyrob.zombiesmash) that was submitted on 18 October and never reviewed. Nothing in the app has changed in a way that affects review.

1. What the app is and who it is for
Zombie Smash is a free, single-player, retro-style arcade shooter. You defend a line at the bottom of the screen, shoot incoming zombies, take on seven boss characters across seven arenas, and try to beat your high score. It is made for casual players aged 12 and over who like short arcade sessions. The violence is cartoon-style zombie shooting only. There are no ads, no accounts and no sign-in. There is no paid content and there are no in-app purchases.

2. How to use it (no login or sample files needed)
Press FIRE on the home screen, type any player name (2 to 24 letters or numbers) and press NEW GAME. You can watch or skip the opening story (tap FIRE to bring in the next comic panel). Slide the joystick left and right to move, hold FIRE to shoot, and tap the blue button to throw a grenade. Zombies arrive from the top and a boss turns up every second wave. If 10 zombies get through, the run ends. MENU pauses the game and opens settings, console skins (including joystick colours), high scores, how to play and the privacy policy.
To test quickly, a name that starts with BOT_ (for example BOT_Reviewer) switches on a cheat mode. MENU then shows a CHEATS page with the codes, which you enter on the console after pressing MENU twice. Runs in this mode never reach the public leaderboard.

3. External services and data
- Supabase (supabase.com) hosts the online high-score list. When a run starts, the app sends the player name and a random identifier made on the device. When the run ends it sends the score, kills, time and wave. The name and score can show on the public leaderboard. Names are checked against a filter for objectionable words, and a name can be removed on request through https://zc2.quest/support.html or support@zc2.quest.
- zc2.quest (web hosting) serves the privacy policy and support pages. The privacy policy is also inside the app (MENU, then PRIVACY POLICY).
The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, social login, payment processor or AI service, and it does not read any hardware or advertising identifier. It works fully offline: scores are saved on the device and uploaded later.

4. Regions and protected material
It works the same in every region, in English, with the same content. The app is not in a regulated industry. The characters, artwork, music and sound effects are original; the boss characters are fictional and inspired by friends' gamer tags, with their blessing, and no one else's protected material is used.
