Seven weird bosses. One line to hold. Zero ads.

Joey Rob is dug in, the horde is coming, and Dr Mantis has brought friends. Zombie Smash is a pocket-sized retro arcade shooter with big personality: simple to pick up, hard to put down, and always good for one more go. Perfect for a spooky season.

HOW IT PLAYS
Slide the stick to move. Hold the big FIRE button to shoot. Tap the grenade button to throw a grenade straight up your lane. Stop the zombies at your line: let 10 through and the camp falls.

MEET THE MISFITS
Fight seven ridiculous bosses with multiple forms, including Disco Man, the Caffeinated Sloth and Dr Mantis himself. Every showdown is told in fully illustrated comic scenes with cheeky dialogue and a nasty surprise or two.

SEVEN LIVING ARENAS
From a burning firehouse square to a neon dance floor that lights up in colour and bursts open as zombies rise through the tiles, every arena has its own look, hazards and entrances.

SPECIAL WEAPONS
Light all three floor targets in an arena to win a temporary boss-themed weapon: a laser pulse, a water pulse, a bass cannon and more.

YOUR ARCADE, YOUR WAY
Play on the grey JW handheld, complete with a glowing LED display, working lamps and chunky arcade buttons, or choose from 24 console skins in all. Beat bosses to unlock the illustrated character skins, each with its own 3D controls and a real arcade-cabinet feel.

KEEP THE RUN ALIVE
Chain kills to build your boost, grab weapon pickups, collect hearts and chase a bigger score. Beat Dr Mantis and the fight carries on in endless survival.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
- Instant to learn: move, fire, grenade
- Seven bosses with multiple forms and illustrated story scenes
- Seven animated arenas with unique zombie entrances
- Boss-themed special weapons
- 24 console skins, eight unlockable character designs
- 16-bit-flavoured music and sound effects
- Weapons, boosts, hearts and endless survival

- Free to play. No ads. No account needed
