Seven weird bosses. One line to hold. Zero ads.

Joey Rob is dug in, the horde is coming, and Dr Mantis has brought friends. Zombie Smash is a pocket-sized retro arcade shooter with big personality: simple to pick up, hard to put down, and always good for one more go.

HOW IT PLAYS
Slide the stick to move. Hold the big red button to fire. Tap the yellow button to throw a grenade straight up your lane. Stop the zombies at your line: let 10 through and the camp falls.

MEET THE MISFITS
Fight seven ridiculous bosses with multiple forms, including Disco Man, the Caffeinated Sloth and Dr Mantis himself. Every showdown is told in fully illustrated story scenes with cheeky dialogue and a nasty surprise or two.

SEVEN LIVING ARENAS
From a burning firehouse square to a neon dance floor that lights up in colour and bursts open as zombies rise through the tiles, every arena has its own look, hazards and entrances.

YOUR ARCADE, YOUR WAY
Choose from 23 console skins. Beat bosses to unlock eight illustrated character skins, each with chunky 3D buttons, glowing lamps and a real arcade-cabinet feel.

KEEP THE RUN ALIVE
Chain kills to build your boost, grab weapon pickups, collect hearts and chase a bigger score. Beat Dr Mantis and the fight carries on in endless survival.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
- Instant to learn: move, fire, grenade
- Seven bosses with multiple forms and illustrated story scenes
- Seven animated arenas with unique zombie entrances
- 23 console skins, eight unlockable character designs
- Weapons, boosts, hearts and endless survival
- Portrait play with a classic handheld feel
- Free to play. No ads. No account needed
- An optional tip jar if you'd like to buy the developer a coffee

A little retro. A little ridiculous. A lot of "one more go".

Pick your name. Hold the line. Zombie Smash.
