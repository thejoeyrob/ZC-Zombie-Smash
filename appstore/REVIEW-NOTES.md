2. What the app is for and who it's for
Zombie Smash is a free, single-player, retro-style arcade shooter. You defend a line at the bottom of the screen, shoot incoming zombies, take on seven boss characters across seven arenas, and try to beat your high score. It's made for casual players aged 12 and over who like short arcade sessions. The violence is cartoon-style zombie shooting only. There are no ads and no sign-in.

3. How to use it
You don't need a login or any sample files. Press FIRE on the home screen, type any player name (2 to 24 letters or numbers) and press NEW GAME. You can watch or skip the opening story (tap FIRE to bring in the next comic panel). Slide the joystick left and right to move, hold FIRE to shoot, and tap the blue button to throw a grenade. Zombies arrive from the top and a boss turns up every second wave. If 10 zombies get through, the run ends. MENU pauses the game and opens settings, console skins (including joystick colours), high scores, how to play and the privacy policy.
If you want to test things quickly, a name that starts with BOT_ (for example BOT_Reviewer) switches on a cheat mode. MENU then shows a CHEATS page with the codes, which you enter on the console after pressing MENU twice. Runs in this mode never reach the public leaderboard.

4. External services
- Supabase (supabase.com) hosts the online high-score list. When a run starts, the app sends the player name and a random identifier made on the device. When the run ends it sends the score, kills, time and wave. The name and score can show on the public leaderboard. Names are checked against a filter for objectionable words, and a name can be removed on request through https://zc2.quest/support.html.
- zc2.quest (web hosting) only serves the privacy policy and support pages. The privacy policy is also inside the app (MENU, then PRIVACY POLICY).
The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, social login, payment processor or AI service, and it doesn't read any hardware or advertising identifier. It works fully offline: scores are saved on the device and uploaded later.

5. Regional differences
None. It works the same in every region, in English, with the same content.
