# Art style for Fatty's Game Shack (owner's standard)

The owner picked this as "the level I want" for logos, store art and any
future artwork: `store-art/reference/logo-style-target.png`.

What that means in practice:

- **Glowing neon, not flat.** Pink (magenta) outer ring and cyan inner ring drawn as
  lit tubes with a soft bloom, on a near-black navy ground.
- **Glossy, 3D lettering.** The title is chunky italic, gold-to-yellow with a bright
  gloss highlight, a thick dark-purple outline and a drop shadow. Not flat fill.
- **Hot colour burst behind the title.** A spiky orange-to-yellow starburst with a lit edge.
- **Banner and props are polished.** Magenta ribbon with white italic text and a dark
  outline; a glossy arcade joystick (yellow ball, red and blue buttons) on a base
  lit in cyan, standing on a purple synthwave grid.
- **Tagline follows the curve** of the ring ("EST. 1985 • ZOMBIES WELCOME"), gold with a
  dark outline.
- Rich saturated colour, strong contrast, depth and lighting. Avoid plain flat vector,
  muted greys and thin outlines unless the owner asks for that specific look
  (the grey brushed-metal door-logo variant was a one-off request).

The in-game door-window logo (`vr-art/fatty-logo.png`, `fatty-decal.png`, made by
`tools/make_signs.py`) is a flatter earlier version of the same badge; new store art
and signage should be raised to the level of the reference above.

Meta store art sizes already generated in `store-art/`: 2560x1440 cover, 1440x1440
square, 1008x1344 portrait, 3000x900 hero, 512x512 icon (24-bit PNG, no transparency).
