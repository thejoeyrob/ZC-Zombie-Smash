Zombie Smash – Support

Having trouble with the game, found a bug or want a name removed from the leaderboard? Tell us and we will help.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/thejoeyrob/ZC-Zombie-Smash/issues. Please do not post anything private there.

Quick fixes

  • No sound: check the silent switch and your volume, then open the in-game menu and look at Music & Sound.
  • Leaderboard will not load: it needs an internet connection. The game still plays offline and sends your score when you are back online.
  • Game looks wrong after an update: close the app completely and open it again.
