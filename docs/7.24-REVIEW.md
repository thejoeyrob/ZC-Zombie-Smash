# Zombie Smash 7.24.0 — visual campaign review

Built on chatgpt-pro-build commit bba66467ecb768bf1475de5e05c986c5f2725c95. Existing Debo music fixes, controls, unlocks, cheats and original three intro story slides are retained.

## What changed

- 30 new original comic illustrations replace boss entrance, mutation, victory and defeat videos. Dialogue stays legible above an accessible Continue button; timed progression and pause remain supported. Illustrations use gentle horizontal movement and drifting light; no character warping or layered 3D video.
- Seven brighter boss-themed arenas. Each pair of campaign waves uses the upcoming boss's map. Infinite survival cycles through all seven.
- Marked floor access hatches spawn the regular horde on clear ground, below decorative structures. Enemies fan out smoothly into varied lanes before advancing. Larger enemies get extra side clearance. Entry pacing compensates for the shorter visible route; active routes survive save/restore.
- Shoot three floor lamps to activate each arena's rechargeable boost: coolant slow, reactor discharge, chain shock, coffee rapid fire, disco shield, supply shield or harvest purge. Bullets continue through floor targets. These are pinball-inspired targets, not bouncing projectiles.
- Disco Man alternates vinyl records with disco balls. Neon floor lighting changes slowly and is static when reduced motion is enabled. The other maps have subtle perimeter embers, dust, steam or laboratory lights.
- Eight character console skins replaced with new illustrations. All 23 skins share the same immutable screen and control geometry. Source window measurements fit each illustration around that geometry.
- Welcome screen uses Joey's illustrated story artwork with a stable, readable title.
- App-icon chooser and its native build registration removed. Primary app icon retained.
- Personal bests save before waiting for the server. The scores screen shows local records immediately and labels them separately from global results. Retryable failures retain ticketed scores, and concurrent flush requests share one queue drain.
