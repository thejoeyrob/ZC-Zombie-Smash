SHORT WRITE-UP
Zombie Smash is a small game with a big personality: a portrait arcade shooter built around straightforward controls, a wonderfully weird cast and the appeal of one more go. Players join Joey Rob to hold back the horde, take on seven distinctive bosses and chase a better score. From Disco Man to Caffeinated Sloth, the characters bring plenty of mischief, while collectible console finishes give the game the feel of a pocket arcade. Move, fire, time your grenades and enjoy the chaos.
