Big personalities. Simple arcade fun. Just one more go.

The zombies are coming, the bosses are ridiculous, and Joey Rob has a line to hold. Welcome to Zombie Smash: a pocket-sized burst of retro arcade action with a wonderfully weird cast and a proper soft spot for old-school fun.

Move left. Move right. Fire away. Grab a useful weapon, time your grenades and keep the horde from breaking through. The controls are straightforward; beating your last run is where things get interesting.

MEET THE MISFITS
Take on seven distinctive bosses, including the gloriously over-the-top Disco Man, the suspiciously speedy Caffeinated Sloth and Dr Mantis himself. Expect big personalities, cheeky dialogue and more than one nasty surprise.

MAKE IT YOUR ARCADE
Choose a console finish to suit your mood. Beat bosses to unlock their character-themed skins and give your pocket arcade a fresh look.

KEEP THE RUN ALIVE
Build your boost, pick up different weapons and chase a better score. Make it past Dr Mantis and the fight continues in endless survival.

- Straightforward left-and-right movement and a dedicated fire button
- Seven bosses with multiple forms
- Weapon pickups, grenades and score multipliers
- Custom console skins and boss-themed unlocks
- Portrait play with a classic handheld-console feel
- Play without an account; online leaderboards need an internet connection

A little retro. A little ridiculous. A lot of "one more go".

Pick your name. Hold the line. Zombie Smash.
